# HydePark Directory: Strategic Brief & Design Innovation Opportunity

**Date**: April 2026  
**Scope**: Phase 1 MVP positioning + community-first differentiation  
**Question**: Can we build a directory that *feels* different from Yelp/Google Maps by borrowing from adjacent playbooks?

---

## The Insight

**Directories are dead. Communities are thriving.**

- Yelp is search-first; nobody logs in daily to "use" it
- Google Maps is utility-first; pure convenience play
- Discord, Reddit, Mighty Networks, Indie Hackers = people *want* to come back

**HydePark can own the intersection**: A directory that's also a community.

---

## What the Research Revealed

### 1. Successful Directories Are Now Multimedia-First
**Evidence**: Rich media listings get **4.3x more engagement** than text-only  
**Examples**: AIScreen (photos + AR), Brilliant Directories (10K+ users with media-rich onboarding)  
**Application**: HydePark should prioritize photo galleries, user-generated content, video testimonials

### 2. Trust Layers Drive Conversion
**Evidence**: Airbnb's trust stack = profile photos + mutual connections + reviews + reputation  
**Examples**: Mandatory profile photos increase trust; showing "You have 3 mutual friends with this host" creates confidence  
**Application**: 
- Require business owner photos + short bio
- Show "Also studied at UChicago" / "Also lives in HydePark" signals
- Highlight student reviews prominently

### 3. Community Identity > Features
**Evidence**: Discord/Mighty Networks communities thrive on *rituals* and *identity*, not just features  
**Examples**: 
  - Monday/Wednesday/Friday threads (predictable participation)
  - "Community spotlights" (makes members feel like insiders)
  - Leaderboards (top reviewers, most active students)
**Application**: Weekly spotlights, featured reviews, student badges (e.g., "Top Reviewer," "Local Explorer")

### 4. Discussion Depth > Vanity Metrics
**Evidence**: Reddit comment-to-post ratios of 8:1–15:1 indicate health; 82% of Redditors prefer authentic, non-promotional content  
**Application**: Add threaded discussions to business listings. Encourage student Q&A ("Best thing to order?" "Good for study groups?")

### 5. Marketplace Patterns (Airbnb, Etsy) Apply to Local Services
**Evidence**: Visual-first discovery (carousel/swiping) works better than search bars; maker stories build connection  
**Application**: 
- Homepage carousel of "Featured moments" (not just businesses)
- Business owner origin stories ("Why we opened in HydePark")
- Student testimonial features

### 6. Mobile-First is Non-Negotiable
**Evidence**: 81% of "near me" searches happen on mobile; multi-platform discovery is standard  
**Application**: Card-based, swipeable interface; photo-first design; fast load times

## Design Patterns Worth Stealing

| Pattern | From | How to Apply |
|---------|------|--------------|
| **Predictable rituals** | Discord | Monday: new listings, Wed: business tips, Fri: student reviews |
| **Weekly spotlights** | Product Hunt | "Business of the week," "Student reviewer spotlight" |
| **Mutual connections** | Airbnb | "You both went to UChicago," "3 others from your dorm live here" |
| **User reputation badges** | Reddit/Stack Overflow | "Top 5 Reviewer," "Local Explorer," "Community Helper" |
| **Carousel discovery** | Instagram/Etsy | Swipeable featured businesses & photos |
| **Story-first onboarding** | Mighty Networks | Business bios, founder stories, community mission |
| **Discussion threads** | Reddit | Comments on business listings: "Thoughts? Questions?" |
| **AMA format** | Indie Hackers/Reddit | "Ask the founder anything" sessions |
| **Photo-first UGC** | Instagram/Etsy | "Add your photo," student-tagged photos at businesses |
| **Community moderation** | Reddit | Trusted student "mods" who curate quality discussions |

## Positioning Shift: From Search Engine to Community Platform

### Today (Yelp/Google Maps Model)
```
User: "I'm hungry, find me a restaurant"
Platform: Returns search results
Interaction: One-time, transactional
Value: Convenience
```

### Opportunity (HydePark Community Model)
```
User: "What's new in HydePark this week?"
Platform: Shows spotlights, reviews, office hours, community recommendations
Interaction: Recurring, relational
Value: Belonging + Discovery + Recommendations
```

## Phase 1 MVP + Community Layer (Quick Wins)

### High Impact, Low Effort
1. **Discussion threads on business pages** (5–7 days)
   - Simple: "Comments" section per business
   - Ask: "Have you been here? What's your favorite thing?"
   - Encourage authentic, helpful replies

2. **5-star review system with text** (3–5 days)
   - User can rate + leave short review
   - Displayed on business card & detail page
   - Feed/badge for top reviewers

3. **Weekly spotlight carousel** (3–5 days)
   - Homepage feature: "Business of the week" (admin-curated or voting)
   - Includes photo + quote from owner/student review
   - Drives repeat visits

4. **Simple user profiles** (5–7 days)
   - Student: Avatar + username + "reviewed X businesses" + badge
   - Business owner: Photo + short bio + "Asked Y questions" badge
   - Show mutual connections (simple: "You both studied at UChicago")

5. **Photo upload on business detail page** (3–5 days)
   - "Add your photo" button
   - Gallery below listing
   - Social proof via visual content

### Effort-to-Impact Ratio
- **Total effort**: 2–3 weeks of development (1 person)
- **Expected impact**: Shift perception from "directory" → "community"; increase repeat visits by 3–5x

## Differentiation Thesis

**HydePark is where students discover places AND build social capital**

- You get recommendations from people like you (students, locals)
- You contribute reviews and become known as a "trusted voice"
- You see what's new + what's trending in your community
- You feel like you belong to something bigger than just a directory

**Yelp/Google Maps**: "Find what you want"  
**HydePark**: "Find what you want, where people like you shop"

---

## Metrics That Matter

Forget:
- "We have 50 listings"
- "We have 1,000 users"

Track:
- [ ] % of students who leave a review (engagement)
- [ ] Comment-to-listing ratio (discussion depth)
- [ ] Weekly repeat visitors (stickiness)
- [ ] User-generated content volume (ownership/trust)
- [ ] Business response rate to reviews (community health)

---

## Risk Mitigation

### Risk: Discussions get negative/toxic
**Mitigation**: Moderation playbook (clear rules, quick response), student mod program, report system

### Risk: Yelp/Google Maps already have scale
**Mitigation**: We're not competing on search—we're competing on *community*. Different value prop.

### Risk: Business owners don't participate
**Mitigation**: Show them engagement stats in their dashboard; highlight that responsive businesses get more reviews/traffic

## Timeline

**Week 1**: Audit current UI/UX; choose 2–3 features to prioritize  
**Week 2–3**: Build discussion threads + review system + spotlights  
**Week 4**: Gather feedback from 5–10 early testers (students + businesses)

## Next: Build vs. Buy vs. Partner?

**Build** (Recommended): Our codebase gives us flexibility to iterate fast on community features. 2–3 weeks to MVP + community.

**Buy/Integrate**: Mighty Networks, Disco, Hivebrite offer pre-built community + directory. Cost: $50–500/mo. Slower to customize; less control over brand.

**Partner**: Could we white-label Mighty Networks or build a Stripe integration partner? Lower development cost, slower differentiation.

**Recommendation**: **Build Phase 1 MVP + community layer in Next.js.** Fast feedback loops, full control, lean cost. Revisit if community needs exceed our capacity.

---

## Closing Thoughts

Directories aren't dead—but static directories are dead. The opportunity is to build a *network effect*: each student who leaves a review makes the platform more valuable for the next student. Each business that participates makes it more valuable for the next business.

That's the HydePark differentiation. Not "find local businesses." But "find local businesses *and* be part of a community that values authenticity, recommendations from people like you, and local discovery.*"

**Let's make it feel like something new.**
